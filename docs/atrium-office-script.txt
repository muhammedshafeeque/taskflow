ATRIUM - HOW THE OFFICE WORKS, END TO END
Full role-by-role script, with a gap list

Every step says who does it, where in Atrium, what they do, and what should
happen. [GAP n] points to the numbered list in Part 4. Where a gap blocks a
step, a workaround is given. People, companies and numbers are invented samples.

General level rule, for every team:

  Intern   Learns: views, works only on assigned items, approves nothing.
  Junior   Does: creates and updates own work. No delete, no approval.
  Senior   Does and checks: wider view, reviews juniors, trains interns.
  Lead     Runs the team: assigns, approves, sees team reports.
  Manager  Runs the function: policies, budget, cross-team decisions.


THE STORY IN ONE PARAGRAPH

Adam sets up the company and the first HR person, Hana. Hana builds the HR team
and the policies, then hires the executives and every team, each with a lead,
seniors, juniors and interns. Adam gives each person the role for their team
and level. IT and procurement equip them. Marketing brings leads, sales
converts and closes one, and hands it to delivery. The BA team writes the
requirements, design, development and QA build it, and DevOps releases it.
Support and account management look after the customer, accounts bills it, HR
runs payroll and careers, and the executives review the business.

SCENE 1. SYSTEM ADMIN SETS UP THE COMPANY
Who: Adam

 1.1 Sign in at /login. The home hub shows the module tiles.
 1.2 Core > Company: enter company name, logo and base currency.
 1.3 Core > Currencies and Exchange rates: add the currencies you trade in.
 1.4 Core > Modules: switch on Project Manager, Resources, CRM, Contracts,
     Billing, Accounts, HRMS, Assets, Procurement, Service Desk, Customer
     Portal, Documents, Calendar and Monitor.
     Expect: a disabled module disappears for everyone.
 1.5 Roles: create every role in Part 2. The seed provides only Super Admin,
     Project Manager, Developer, Viewer, Org Manager, Sales, Account Manager,
     Support Agent and CRM Admin [GAP 1].
     Each user has exactly ONE role, so each team and level pair is its own
     role, for example "Sales - Junior" and "Sales - Lead" [GAP 29].


SCENE 2. THE FIRST HR AND THE HR TEAM
Who: Adam, then Hana

 2.1 Adam: Users > Add user: Hana, work email, role HR - Manager.
     Expect: an invite email with a set-password link, valid for 24 hours.
 2.2 Adam: HRMS > Employees > + Add employee: Hana, SAME email, department
     HR, designation "HR Manager", status Active, joining date, annual CTC.
     This is separate from 2.1 because the two are not connected [GAP 4].
 2.3 Hana sets a password, signs in and opens Me (HR self-service).
     Expect: the employee record links to the account by matching email.
 2.4 Hana adds Carl (CEO) as an employee; Adam adds Carl's account, role CEO.
 2.5 Hana hires Helen, Harry, Hugo and Hiro with the steps of Scene 5. Adam
     adds their accounts: HR - Senior, HR - Payroll, HR - Junior, HR - Intern.
 2.6 Split the HR work:

       Hana    Policies, offers, salaries, payroll check, final approvals
       Helen   Recruitment, interviews, onboarding, appraisals, exits
       Harry   Attendance, leave, payroll preparation
       Hugo    Candidate entry, onboarding follow-up, attendance entry
       Hiro    Candidate list and onboarding view, learns only

 2.7 Check: Hugo cannot open Payroll, Hiro cannot change anything, Helen
     cannot change leave policy, Harry cannot open Recruitment.
     Salary is still returned in the employee data to Hugo [GAP 30].


SCENE 3. HR SETS THE POLICIES
Who: Hana

 3.1 HRMS > HR settings: weekend days, leave-year start month, hours per day
     (8), working days per year (260), weekly hours (40), timesheet reminder
     day, recruitment stages, and the leave approvers (Hana and Harry).
 3.2 HRMS > Leave types: create

       Casual   paid     12 days   monthly accrual   carry forward 0
       Sick     paid     10 days   yearly            carry forward 0
       Earned   paid     15 days   monthly accrual   carry forward 10
       Unpaid   unpaid    0 days   reduces pay

     Expect: allowances are pro-rated for people who join mid-year.
 3.3 HRMS > Holidays: add this year's holidays, using Location for branches.
     Expect: leave day counts skip weekends and holidays.
 3.4 HRMS > Payroll: set the pay components. Default is Basic 50% of CTC,
     HRA 40% of Basic, Special allowance 30% of CTC. Add deductions such as
     tax or provident fund. Set working days per month (22).
 3.5 HRMS > On/offboarding: make three onboarding templates (intern, staff,
     manager) and one offboarding template. Assign each task to HR, the
     manager or the employee.
 3.6 HRMS > Appraisals: create a review cycle with questions and a rating
     scale. Leave it as draft.
 3.7 Documents > Policies: handbook, leave, attendance, code of conduct,
     information security and remote work.
     Nobody is asked to read and accept them [GAP 5].

       Intern    HR round

11.2 Lena checks the quote. Sara emails it to Omar and moves the deal to
     Proposal, then Negotiation.
11.3 Sara revises the sent quote once. Expect: the version goes up.
11.4 Omar agrees by email. Sara marks the quote Accepted. Omar has no accept

     No draft invoice is created for portal customers [GAP 14].
11.5 Fiona checks the payment terms. Lena activates the contract. Bela sends
     the SOW from Documents for e-signature.
11.6 Sara moves the deal to Won. Accepting the quote does not do it [GAP 13].

     commission of 2,500 appears.
11.7 Farah approves the commission.
11.8 Lena marks any dead deal Lost with a reason. Victor reviews the forecast.

PART 4 - GAPS FOUND
======================================================================

Type:  BUG = wrong result   LOGIC = business rule missing
       FEATURE = not built  SECURITY = access risk

--- FIX FIRST ---

GAP 2   SECURITY  Any user with "update user" can make another user an
        admin, or grant any permission to anyone, including themselves. The
        server does not check that the giver holds those rights.
        Fix: block granting above your own rights, and log every change.

GAP 7   BUG  The Hire button always fails. The screen sends only a joining
        date; the server requires an employee code.
        Fix: generate the code, and ask for CTC, title and onboarding
        template in a proper form.

GAP 8   LOGIC  Reporting manager cannot be set on any screen, yet leave,
        timesheet, appraisal and onboarding routing depend on it. Phone,
        employee code and currency are missing from the form too.
        Fix: add a manager picker and an org chart. Also, a manager is
        notified of leave but can decide only with the company-wide leave
        permission; allow it for direct reports.

GAP 14  BUG  Accepting a quote creates a draft invoice only when the quote
        has a CRM Account. Lead conversion creates a portal organisation
        instead, so the normal sales path yields no invoice.
        Fix: use one customer record, or create the Account on conversion.

GAP 17  BUG  Loss of pay is counted twice when a day is both approved unpaid
        leave and marked absent.
        Fix: count each employee and date once; write approved leave into
        attendance.

GAP 18  BUG  Payroll pays a full month to people who join or leave mid-month.
        Terminated people are excluded even when the exit date is in that
        month. Currencies are added together in one total.
        Fix: pro-rate by dates, include final settlement, total per currency.

GAP 20  SECURITY  Setting an employee to Terminated does not disable the
        login, and nothing reassigns their leads, deals, tasks or approvals.
        Fix: an exit flow that does both on the last working day.

--- TEAMS, LEVELS AND ACCESS ---

GAP 29  LOGIC  No link between team, level and permissions. A user has one
        role. Team lead, seniority and designation are labels only, so every
        team and level pair needs its own role (over 40 here). Roles cannot
        inherit from each other. There is no mentor field.
        Fix: build permissions from team + level, with role inheritance.

GAP 30  SECURITY  The employee list API returns the full record, salary
        included, to anyone with employee list, read or HR dashboard access.
        Fix: require a separate salary permission.

GAP 31  LOGIC  HR access is company-wide. A lead cannot be limited to their
        own team's employees, candidates, leave or attendance. Timesheet
        approve also covers the whole workspace, not only the lead's team.
        Fix: own / team / department / all scope on every permission.
        This also solves GAP 10.

GAP 32  FEATURE  Project designations start with only Lead and Member.
        Fix: company-wide designation templates for Tech Lead, Senior,
        Junior, Intern, QA and Designer, suggested from the employee's level.

GAP 33  FEATURE  No promotion, transfer or intern-conversion flow. One change
        must be made in four places, with no approval, effective date or
        history.

--- APPROVALS AND OTHER TEAMS ---

GAP 34  LOGIC  Purchase order status can move from any state to any other,
        so approval can be skipped, and there is no amount limit, second
        approver, or check that the creator is not the approver.

GAP 35  LOGIC  An expense editor can approve their own claim; no limit or
        second approver. Expense update and approve are one permission.

GAP 36  FEATURE  Not found: requirement sign-off and change-request approval
        with cost and date impact, for the BA team.

GAP 37  FEATURE  No L1/L2/L3 support levels or automatic escalation found in
        the Service Desk code.

GAP 38  SECURITY  The admin user type always receives every permission, so
        an IT admin can see payroll and finance. There is no read-only
        executive or limited-admin type.

GAP 39  FEATURE  No shared approval engine. Each module approves in its own
        way, so "lead, then head, then CEO by amount" cannot be set up, and
        there is no delegate while an approver is on leave.

--- SALES ---

GAP 3   SECURITY  The seeded Sales role holds every CRM permission, including
        pipeline settings, delete and commission approval. A salesperson can
        approve their own commission.

GAP 9   LOGIC  No duplicate check on leads (email, phone or company), and no
        duplicate check on customer organisations at conversion.

GAP 10  LOGIC  No record-level access. Everyone with lead access sees and
        edits all leads and deals. Need own / team / all scopes. Automatic
        assignment, territories and targets were not seen either.

GAP 11  LOGIC  A project is created at lead conversion by default, before the
        deal is won, and always at quote acceptance. The actor becomes
        project lead and is booked at 50%. The convert action needs only
        lead update, not project create.
        Fix: create on Won, ask for a PM, check the project permission.
        Also, conversion marks the lead converted BEFORE the customer and
        project steps. If they fail, the lead is stuck and cannot be retried.

GAP 12  LOGIC  No discount limit or quote approval.

GAP 13  LOGIC  The customer cannot accept a quote online. Acceptance does
        not move the deal to Won. Creating a project from an open deal sets
        Won but skips commission, the stage change and the win alert.
        Loss reason is not required on Lost, nor a reason on Unqualified, by
        the server. Lead stages can be jumped in any order.

GAP 19  LOGIC  Approved commission does not flow into payroll.

--- HR ---

GAP 1   FEATURE  The seed has no CEO, CTO, executive, HR, Delivery manager,
        BA, QA, Support engineer lead or Accounts role, and no seeded role
        except Super Admin includes self-service.

GAP 4   FEATURE  User and employee are created separately and matched by
        email. Hiring raises no account request, and an account creates no
        employee. Adding a user who already exists elsewhere ignores the role
        chosen in the invite.

GAP 5   FEATURE  Policies have no read-and-accept tracking or re-acceptance
        on a new version.

GAP 6   FEATURE  No offer letter, salary approval, offer acceptance or
        candidate careers page. No
        thing stops hiring more than the openings.

GAP 21  LOGIC  Deleting an employee is a hard delete that leaves attendance,
        leave and payroll records behind. Archive instead.

GAP 22  LOGIC  HR's own leave form uses fixed types (annual, sick, casual)
        and ignores the configured Leave types, so it deducts from a
        different balance than self-service. The approval list shows only
        "annual" or "unpaid" for configured types.

GAP 23  LOGIC  Leave: overlapping requests are allowed; a user with leave
        permission can approve their own; a decided request can be decided
        again; leave across two leave years is charged to the first; no
        multi-level approval; no comp-off accrual.

GAP 24  BUG  The hourly cost entered when ADDING an employee is not saved.
        Editing the employee afterwards does save it.

GAP 25  LOGIC  Automatic employee codes use headcount + 1, so deleting an
        employee can make the next code clash.

GAP 26  FEATURE  Not found: shifts, overtime, clock in/out or biometric
        import, statutory payroll rules, bank payment file, probation
        confirmation, salary revision history, employee documents, training,
        warnings and grievances, and salary change audit.
        Payroll has no separate approver: one person can generate, finalize
        and mark it paid.

GAP 27  LOGIC  Attendance: HR entries use server-local midnight while
        self-service uses UTC midnight, so one day can be stored twice if the
        server is not on UTC. HR has no bulk marking.

--- DELIVERY AND FINANCE ---

GAP 15  FEATURE  No sales-to-delivery handover checklist or PM acceptance.
        Resource demand does not create a job opening in Recruitment.

GAP 16  LOGIC  Time to invoice falls back to an hourly rate of 100 with no
        warning when no contract or quote rate exists.

GAP 28  LOGIC  Quote acceptance hides errors. If the contract or project
        fails, the quote still shows Accepted and no one is told.
